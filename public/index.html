    How to survive the night

    🔔
    Listen for attacks. A warning pops up when it tries to get in — through a window, door, chimney, drain, hatch… Rush there!
    📖
    Banish it with words. At the entrance, press E (or Interact) and answer the vocabulary spell. Wrong answers let it push closer.
    ⏳
    Beat the timer. Each attack has a timer. If it runs out, it gets in and a ward breaks.
    🧂
    If it gets in, hide in a salt circle until it leaves. If it catches you, the team loses a banishment.
    🎒
    Grab items: salt shakers, planks to board up entrances, adrenaline, spirit compass, lavender charms, blessed candles.
    🏆
    Win by banishing it 10 times before time runs out. Lose if every ward breaks or time runs out.
